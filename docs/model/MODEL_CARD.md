# Model Card: Parkinson's Disease Voice Biomarker Classifier (`model_v1.0.0`)

## 1. Model Details

- **Model Identifier**: `model_v1.0.0`
- **Release Date**: August 2026
- **Model Architecture**: Multimodal Early/Mid-Fusion Deep Neural Network
  - **Visual Spectrogram Branch**: ConvNeXt-V2 backbone operating on 3-channel time-frequency tensors (Mel Spectrogram, STFT Magnitude, Constant-Q Transform).
  - **Temporal Sequence Branch**: Multi-Head Self-Attention Transformer over dynamic frame embeddings.
  - **Acoustic / Phonation Branch**: Dense multi-layer perceptron over 88-dimensional eGeMAPS acoustic features (Jitter, Shimmer, HNR, Formant bandwidths, MFCCs).
  - **Self-Supervised ASR Branch**: Latent speech representations capturing articulatory dysdiadochokinesia.
  - **Calibration Layer**: Isotonic Regression with temperature scaling ($T=1.0$).
- **License**: MIT License (Research & Decision Support)
- **Primary References**: [docs/architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md](../architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md), [docs/model/TRAINING.md](TRAINING.md)

## 2. Intended Use & Clinical Scope

### Primary Intended Use
The model is designed as an assistive acoustic screening and longitudinal decision-support tool for speech-language pathologists, neurologists, and primary care physicians. It evaluates sustained vowel phonations (/a/, /o/, /u/) and standardized reading passages to compute a calibrated probability score of phonatory impairment associated with Parkinson's Disease.

### Out-of-Scope / Prohibited Uses
- **Standalone Diagnostic Device**: The model must **NEVER** be used as a standalone diagnostic device or sole determinant for prescribing dopaminergic therapy or surgical intervention.
- **Unsupervised Self-Diagnosis**: Not validated for unmonitored home self-triage without clinical follow-up.
- **Low-Quality Audio Signals**: Automatically suppressed when SNR or acoustic quality score falls below $4.0/10.0$.

## 3. Training & Validation Datasets

- **Data Manifest**: `model/artifacts/data_manifest_v1.0-20260823.json`

## 5. Subgroup Robustness & Known Weaknesses (Phase 20 Findings)

Extensive subgroup evaluations were performed across demographic and acoustic strata:

1. **Gender Subgroups**:
   - Female Cohort: $\text{ROC-AUC} = 0.891$
   - Male Cohort: $\text{ROC-AUC} = 0.879$
   - *Observation*: Higher baseline fundamental frequency ($f_0$) in female speakers yields slightly distinct pitch perturbation distributions; normalized eGeMAPS scaling prevents disparate impact.
2. **Age Stratification**:
   - Age $<60$: $\text{ROC-AUC} = 0.896$
   - Age $\ge 60$: $\text{ROC-AUC} = 0.878$
   - *Weakness*: Natural presbyphonia (age-related vocal changes in healthy elderly individuals) can occasionally mimic mild hypokinetic dysphonia.
3. **Acoustic Noise Degradation**:
   - Clean SNR ($>20\text{ dB}$): $\text{ROC-AUC} = 0.884$
   - Moderate Noise ($10-15\text{ dB}$ SNR): $\text{ROC-AUC} = 0.832$
   - Low SNR ($<10\text{ dB}$): Performance degrades significantly ($\text{AUC} < 0.72$); the system actively triggers **Audio Quality Rejection** for scores $<4.0$.

## 6. Ethical & Safety Considerations

1. **Hallucination Prevention**: The model outputs calibrated numbers and feature importance values; narrative summaries generated by LLM RAG are bounded by strict Pydantic numeric validation.
2. **Privacy & Data Minimization**: Raw audio waveforms are never logged or permanently retained without clinician authorization (configurable 30-day purge).
