# Clinical Evidence Reference: Phonatory Acoustic Biomarkers in Parkinson's Disease (2021)

## Summary & Key Findings
This reference discusses speech and phonatory acoustic biomarkers for Parkinson's disease motor speech disorder (hypokinetic dysarthria). It focuses on perturbation and spectral metrics including jitter, shimmer, harmonics-to-noise ratio (HNR), and formant resonance stability.

Key clinical findings:
1. Increased fundamental frequency perturbation (jitter) and amplitude perturbation (shimmer) indicate vocal fold vibratory instability.
2. Decreased HNR correlates with vocal breathiness and incomplete glottic closure.
3. Formant centralization and speech rate reductions correlate with articulatory dysdiadochokinesia and MDS-UPDRS Part III motor ratings.
